## Weaknesses, ordered

- **W1** nothing originates work — no `kind: nomination` memo, no provenance.
- **W2** nothing carries a conversation into a brief — `brief.draft` still sends only a title.
- **W3** no reviewer — D4/D5 unbuilt, so audit tier 2 has no primitive to share.
- **W4** tier 0 is flat; `verify:` is not keyed to criteria, so one failing command disputes every claim.
- **W5** supersede inherits decisions only; brief #2's HLD/LLD are template stubs.
- **W6** `rerule` clears the staleness note without anything re-ruling — the gate can be cleared cheaply.
- **W7** staleness is one level deep; no transitive closure over `dependsOn`.
- **W8** overriding `hld-1` staled two dependents, not the spec's "exactly one". Nothing bounds the cost.
- **W9** an expanded decision overflows the 38vh drawer it lives in.
- **W10** the override line records no timestamp and no author.

W7 is the cheapest real fix and the only silent one.
